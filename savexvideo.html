How it works FAQ

Download X videos in HD

Paste the link of any public post, choose a quality and save the MP4. Free, no signup, nothing to install.

Three steps, about ten seconds

  1. Copy the post link

    On X, tap Share on the post, then Copy link. On desktop, copy the address from the browser bar.

  2. Paste it above

    Paste the link in the field and press Download. We fetch every quality available for that video.

  3. Save the MP4

    Pick the resolution you want. The file goes straight to your phone or computer.

Questions people ask

Is the X video downloader free?

Yes. There is no limit and no registration.

Where are downloaded videos saved?

In your browser's usual Downloads folder. On iPhone, open the Files app, then Downloads.

Can I download from a private account?

No. Only videos from public posts can be fetched.

Is it legal to download videos?

Videos belong to their creators. Use downloads for personal use, and ask permission or give credit before you republish anything.
