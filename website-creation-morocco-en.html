FAQ

Website creation in Morocco: your questions

How much does a website cost in Morocco for a tourism business?

A professional tourism website in Morocco typically starts around 4,500 MAD for a showcase site and rises to 16,000 MAD or more with a booking engine and online payments. The price depends on the number of pages, languages and features. ProBoost Agency sends a fixed quote after a free briefing.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most tourism websites are delivered in 3 to 6 weeks, from discovery to launch. Simple showcase sites are faster, while sites with booking and payments need more time.

Can the website take bookings and payments?

Yes. We integrate booking forms, availability calendars and payment solutions. The available payment options depend on your company structure and your bank, which we review at the start of the project.

Will my website rank first on Google?

Nobody can honestly guarantee a specific position. We build the technical, local and content foundations that make ranking possible, then track progress with monthly SEO reports. Results usually build over several months.

Which languages can the website be in?

French and English are standard, with separate pages and hreflang tags. Arabic and Spanish can be added on request.

What do I need to provide to start?

Your logo, photos, a short description of your services and prices. If you are missing something, we can help with copywriting and photo direction.
