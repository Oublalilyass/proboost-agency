Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about website costs, our guides and free tools.

How much does a website cost in Morocco for a tourism business?

A professional tourism website in Morocco typically starts around 4,500 MAD for a showcase site, around 9,000 MAD for a full site with local SEO, and 16,000 MAD or more with online booking and payments. Domain and hosting are billed separately. Use our free estimator on the website prices page for a range based on your needs.

Which articles should a restaurant owner read first?

Start with the guide to restaurant websites in Morocco, then read the city guide for Marrakech or Agadir. The Agadir guide includes a free calculator that estimates the cost of a restaurant website in MAD and how long it could take to pay for itself.

Are the calculators and tools on this blog free?

Yes. The Airbnb revenue calculators, the direct booking savings calculator, the SEO audit tool and the website cost estimators are free and need no signup. They give indicative estimates only, not guaranteed results.

Where does ProBoost Agency work?

ProBoost Agency is based in Marrakech and works with tourism businesses across Morocco, including Agadir, Fès, Ifrane, Azrou, Essaouira and Tanger. Projects are managed remotely by WhatsApp, phone, email and video call, and most websites are delivered in 3 to 6 weeks.
